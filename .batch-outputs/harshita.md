The ninth lunar day, Navami, signals the end of a cycle, while the Pushya star lights a path of nourishment and learning, urging you to share wisdom. Thursday’s spirit of teaching, the Siddha yoga of mastery, and the Taitila karana of transformation weave together a tapestry of completion, responsibility, and new beginnings. Your Splenic authority whispers present‑moment guidance, and your 1/4 profile blends pioneering independence with a natural pull toward collaboration, all while your split definition reminds you that full expression blooms through partnership.
